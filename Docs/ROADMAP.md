# Roadmap

## M1 — LiveState Playground
Pure Swift state/event/reducer architecture, objective evaluation contract, policy actions, checkpoints, branching, tests, typed playground.

## M2 — Realtime Voice Playground
Audio session, voice activity, transcription, streaming speech, silence, barge-in, cancellation and recovery.

## M3 — Branch Playground
Persisted checkpoint graph, retry, branch comparison, deterministic event-log replay.

## M4 — Duo Playground
Adaptive device layer, hinge/configuration experiments, reserved-region layouts, seated encounter, open reflection, camera accessory experiments where supported.

## M5 — Production App
Interview vertical, onboarding, reflection/progress, privacy, persistence/backend, accessibility, CI and App Store foundations.
